# Casimir-to-disk ladder rule

Tier  of  the identification:  `[O]`.  Tier of  the  arithmetic  below: `[D]`,  from `casimir_disk_ladder.py`.

The  rule  uses one  length.  The acceleration  given  to  a surface  density Σ by the Casimir pressure of a cavity of that size is

a(L) = π² ℏ c / (240 L⁴ Σ).

A  single  ladder would  require  a(L) =  a₀  when  L is  a galactic length and Σ is the disk's own surface density. With Σ = 100 M⊙ pc⁻² and the frozen SPARC a₀ = 1.116×10⁻¹⁰ m s⁻², that equality holds at L = 8.64×10⁻⁵ m. At 1 kpc the same expression gives a/a₀ = 6.2×10⁻⁹⁵. At 4.3 kpc it gives 1.8×10⁻⁹⁷.

The  plate  law and  the  disk law  do  not  meet. The  galaxy formula has G and a₀. The plate formula has ℏ and c. Sharing only c is not a scaling rule. No action was varied to produce this identification.
