Far out, the two laws are the same law. A flat rotation curve cannot tell them apart. The split is largest right where SPARC looks, around the galaxy scale. In the solar system both have finished turning over, but they finish differently. G.O.D. leaves a constant shove the size of the galaxy scale. ITT leaves a shove hundreds of millions of times smaller, and then fails a different test: the Milky Way's tide, which Cassini did not see.

## What each theory can carry on its own

G.O.D.'s robust piece is the algebra of the dual-channel potential, not a force law for planets or a claim about the expansion. ITT's robust piece is the turnover shape, which matches that derivative and is the function SPARC was rerun on. Neither robust piece is the horizon formula, and neither one passes the solar system without an extra screen.

The hidden connection is the outer galaxy. There the two theories are not two theories. The place they come apart is the turnover and, after that, the solar system. A test that only looks at the outer disk will always say they agree. That agreement is real, and it does not cancel Cassini.
