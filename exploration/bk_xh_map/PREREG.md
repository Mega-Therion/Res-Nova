# Pre-registration: does the Berezhiani–Khoury condensation criterion reproduce the x_H virial cut? (2026-11-19, before any number)

**The two criteria.**
- **x_H screen:** MOND is on where x_H = ‖∇∇Φ_N‖/H² ≥ 154. For a uniform sphere x_H = (√3/2)ρ/ρ_crit, so this is a pure density threshold, ρ ≥ 178ρ_crit, independent of velocity dispersion.
- **BK superfluid** (Berezhiani & Khoury 2015): condensation where the de Broglie wavelength exceeds the interparticle spacing, T < T_c = (2πħ²/mk_B)(n/ζ(3/2))^{2/3}, with T ≈ mσ²/k_B and n = ρ/m. This gives ρ ≥ ρ_BK(σ) = ζ(3/2)·m·(m²σ²/2πħ²)^{3/2} ∝ m⁴σ³.

**Questions, answered by calculation.**
- **Q1.** Is there an m for which ρ_BK(σ) = 178ρ_crit at galactic σ (50–250 km/s)? Report σ_c(m) at the virial density.
- **Q2.** Do the two criteria agree for clusters (σ ≈ 800–1200 km/s)?
- **Q3.** What does the disagreement imply for the tidal-AeST candidate?

**Decision.**
- **Maps** if a single m makes the two criteria agree across galaxies and clusters (within a factor of 2 in ρ).
- **Partial** if they agree only below some σ_c.
- **No map** otherwise.
