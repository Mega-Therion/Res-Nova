## Verdict `[D]`
The paper's conclusion holds, and it is stronger than the paper states. It also has two errors that do not change the result.

| Item | Result |
|---|---|
| Sign rule | Net weight := −1 + β. The brane piece is −1, the same thing B4 in [PHASE3_WALL_GHOST_COUPLED.json](file:///home/mega/Chyren/3_research/Res-Nova/exploration/bulk5d/PHASE3_WALL_GHOST_COUPLED.json) shows. Abelian DBI has ΔH ∝ F, so β = 2 and the weight is +1. Every su(3) generator is traceless (checked), so B·Tr F has no linear gluon term, β = 0, and the weight is −1 at leading order in α′. |
| Proton | A_q − A_g = 0.009 ± 0.037. The deviation 0.991 reproduces the paper. It is robust: \|1 − m_g/m_i\| = 2f_g ≥ 0.60 for \|c̄_g\| ≤ 0.2. At the ~11 TeV string scale, A_g → 16/(16+3n_f) = 0.47–0.52. The exact 0.009 is not robust: the split is scheme-dependent, c̄ was left out, and the central value is consistent with zero. |
| Electroweak (paper error 1) | At rest, a massive vector has equal field-strength and mass-term energy. Under the paper's own rule (−T_SU(2) + T_Higgs), m_grav(W, Z) = 0, not m_W. The photon weight is cos 2θ_W ≈ 0.54. The paper diagonalised only the inertial mass matrix. |
| Wrong test (paper error 2) | Brane matter follows geodesics of the induced metric, so free fall stays universal. MICROSCOPE tests passive mass and is not the right test. The Si-28 comparison is baryons against baryons, and a universal factor would be absorbed into G₄. The violation is in **active** mass. |
| Active mass, Al/Fe | Nuclear Coulomb energy (abelian, weight +1) differs from QCD binding (weight ≈ 0). That gives η_active ≥ 9×10⁻³ across the whole LQCD band, with either electron sign. The LLR bound is 3.9×10⁻¹⁴ (Singh et al. 2023; 4×10⁻¹² from Bartlett–Van Buren 1986). The model is ruled out by **about 11 orders of magnitude**. |
| Res-Nova seat | Minimal coupling to one metric gives every sector weight 1, so η_active = 0 identically. |

## What this means for bulk5d
- [PHASE2_MATTER_SECTOR.json](file:///home/mega/Chyren/3_research/Res-Nova/exploration/bulk5d/PHASE2_MATTER_SECTOR.json)'s +G₄ result holds for stretched-string endpoint dust only. SM baryons are not stretched strings.

- The drag condensate sitting in the −G₄ seat is fine for a dark component. It does not help the baryons.

## Still open `[O]`
1. A mechanism other than B/C₂ that couples non-abelian fields linearly to a bulk mode (for example non-normalizable boundary conditions, arXiv:2311.16242).
2. The quark sign. The paper assumes +1 without deriving it. Brane-localized fermions would naively be −1.
3. Whether α′ corrections (Tr F³, Tr F⁴) can flip the sign. The paper argues they cannot. Not re-derived here.
