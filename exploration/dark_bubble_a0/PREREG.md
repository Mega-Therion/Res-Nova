# Pre-registration: Dark Bubble wall acceleration → a₀ (2026-11-19, written before any number)

**Claim under test.** In the Dark Bubble model, our universe is a 3-brane accelerating through AdS₅. Its 5D proper acceleration a₅ gives a Deser–Levin temperature T = ħ√(a₅² − k²)/(2πck_B). The excess scale a_w ≡ c√(a₅² − k²), combined with a local 4-acceleration g, gives T(g) ∝ √(g² + a_w²). That fixes a MOND scale with no free dial.

**Method.**
- Compute a₅ exactly as the normal (extrinsic, K_ττ) acceleration of a wall trajectory R(τ) in AdS₅ that follows the observed flat ΛCDM history (Planck 2018: H₀ = 67.4, Ω_m = 0.315).
- Bulk is pure AdS on each side. **Stated assumption:** the strings that model matter do not change K_ττ at leading order.
- Two rules map T(g) to a force law, and both are reported:
  - **(R1)** μ = dT/dg ∝ g/√(g² + a_w²) = μ_std(g/a_w), so a₀ = a_w;
  - **(R2)** Milgrom 1999: μ = [T(g) − T(0)]/(…), so a₀ = a_w/2.

**Kill criteria, fixed in advance.**
- **K1 (scale).** a₀ at z = 0 must fall in the SPARC 95% CI [0.972, 1.295]×10⁻¹⁰ m s⁻² under at least one rule, with Ω_m inside Planck 2σ (0.301–0.329). Otherwise [X].
- **K2 (stability over SPARC's volume).** If a_w depends on z, the predicted a₀ across SPARC distances (z ≤ 0.03) must not worsen the radial-acceleration-relation χ² by more than 25 relative to a constant best-fit a₀. Fixed Υ_disk = 0.5, Υ_bulge = 0.7; errors from e_V. Otherwise [X].
- **K3 (Cassini).** Report whether the mechanism is modified inertia or modified gravity, and its residual at Saturn compared with the Cassini/ephemeris bound on an anomalous acceleration (~10⁻¹⁴ m s⁻²).
- **K4 (WEP).** Report whether the derivation uses brane gauge couplings. If it does, it inherits Basile–Borys–Masias.
