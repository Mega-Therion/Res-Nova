# Repository promotion

Chyren is the workshop and the book. A note stays there until it can pass exactly one public check.

**This repository, Res-Nova,** is the only place a sentence may claim to be about the physical world. It enters when it names a catalog, a measurement, or a physical model, and when the mathematics is tagged as mathematics. A Lean proof certifies the mathematics only. Killed claims stay here so the record of what failed remains part of the physics.

**Nova-Conscientia** is the only place a sentence may claim a runtime result: navigator, swarm, clip, charter, tests. A formula borrowed from physics is code. It is not a galaxy test and it is not a second Res-Nova.

Promotion is one way. A note moves from Chyren into one of those two repositories. The other may link to it. It does not grow its own copy of the claim.

On 2026-10-04 the bridge folder and the corpus were reviewed. Forty-four auto-generated bridge notes are not claims. The CMB linear note is already represented by `01_foundational_action/PRD_supplementary/Cosmological_Perturbations.md`. Mass-prediction and Law-of-G.O.D. material stays out. Eighteen workshop notes, including the Freeman-density bridge and the holonomy essays, stay in Chyren until each one names a catalog or a measurement of its own. The Freeman literature baseline is already recorded in this repository's archive audit.
