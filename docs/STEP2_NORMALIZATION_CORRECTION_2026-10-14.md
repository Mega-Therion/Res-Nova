# Step 2 normalization correction

**Date:** 2026-10-14  
**Status:** Documentation correction. No model adoption, branch selection, or theory-tier promotion.

## Corrected setting

Let \(\widehat g\) be the acceleration generated by the bare Newtonian coupling and \(g_N\) the acceleration generated by the measured Newton constant. In the restricted spherical, aligned, quasistatic weak-field regime with negligible AeST mass term,

\[
g=\widehat g+g_\varphi,
\]

\[
\widehat g=\eta g_N,
\]

\[
\eta=\frac{\widehat G}{G_N}=\frac{\lambda_s}{1+\lambda_s}.
\]

The scalar Gauss law is

\[
\mathcal J'(\mathcal Y)g_\varphi=\widehat g=\eta g_N.
\]

## Corrected nondimensional variables

\[
x=\frac g{a_0},
\qquad
y=\frac{g_N}{a_0}=x\mu_{\rm std}(x),
\qquad
s=\frac{g_\varphi}{a_0}.
\]

For the desired total law,

\[
\mu_{\rm std}(x)=\frac{x}{\sqrt{1+x^2}}.
\]

Then

\[
s(x)=x-\eta y=x\left[1-\eta\mu_{\rm std}(x)\right].
\]

## Corrected scalar-channel function

\[
\mathcal J'(a_0^2s^2)
=
\frac{\eta\mu_{\rm std}(x)}{1-\eta\mu_{\rm std}(x)},
\]

\[
\mathcal J(\mathcal Y(x))
=
a_0^2\left[2\eta F_{\rm std}(x)-\eta^2y(x)^2\right],
\]

\[
\mathcal Y(x)=a_0^2s(x)^2,
\]

\[
F_{\rm std}(x)=\frac12\left[x\sqrt{1+x^2}-\operatorname{arsinh}x\right].
\]

## Corrected limits

Deep MOND:

\[
\mathcal J\sim\frac{2\lambda_s}{3\widetilde a_0}\mathcal Y^{3/2},
\qquad
\widetilde a_0=(1+\lambda_s)a_0.
\]

Newtonian end:

\[
\mathcal J'\to\lambda_s.
\]

Total acceleration:

\[
\frac g{g_N}\to1.
\]

Scalar contribution:

\[
\frac{g_\varphi}{g}\to1-\eta.
\]

The scalar contribution does not vanish for finite \(\lambda_s\).
