# Res Nova: The Book

A popular science book narrating a corpus of ideas — from first principles to frontier physics.

## Structure

**Part I: How We Think** (Chapters 1-4)
The architecture of reasoning — from ancient axioms to modern mental models.

**Part II: The Physics of Decisions** (Chapters 5-8)
Physics concepts that double as mental models for life.

**Part III: The Mathematical Universe** (Chapters 9-10)
The hidden shapes that govern everything from stock markets to ocean waves.

**Part IV: The Frontier** (Chapters 11-12)
Where physics breaks down and something new begins.

**Part V: Res Nova** (Chapters 13-15)
The meta-narrative: how knowledge itself grows, connects, and evolves.

## Status
First draft. ~30,000 words. Written for an educated layperson — no equations in the main text.
