# Res Nova: The Book

A popular science book narrating the corpus of ideas behind the Res Nova research program — from first principles to modified gravity and machine-checked physics.

## Structure

**Part I: How We Think** (Chapters 1-4)
The architecture of reasoning — from ancient Greek axioms to modern mental models, inversion, probabilistic thinking, and circles of competence.

**Part II: The Physics of Decisions** (Chapters 5-8)
Physics concepts as mental models: velocity vs. speed, leverage, critical mass, entropy, relativity of perspective.

**Part III: The Mathematical Universe** (Chapters 9-10)
Bell curves vs. power laws — why the world is more unequal than we think. Nonlinear wave dynamics and the limits of prediction.

**Part IV: The Actual Frontier** (Chapters 11-13)
The dark matter problem, the dual-channel variational identity F(x) = x²/2 - x + ln(1+x), and the SPARC measurement of 171 galaxies.

**Part V: Res Nova** (Chapters 14-15)
Machine-checked physics (Lean 4 formalization, the epistemic covenant), and the closing meta-narrative connecting the research program to the first question: what is everything made of?

## Status
First draft. ~30,000 words. Written for an educated layperson — no equations in the main text.
