Project: Res-Nova · R.W. Yett. Every number on this page is regenerated by `./reproduce_core_results.sh` and checked against the committed result files. A cold re-run from a fresh clone on 2026-10-08, on the machine that wrote the files, reproduced all three files exactly (`VERIFICATION_RUN_010_COLD_CORE/`).

Since 2026-10-09 the same command also covers the other headline files. It fetches the SPARC master table, verified by SHA-256, and reruns:
- the strict unit-M/L check (`SPARC_175_summary.json`, `SPARC_175_GOD_fits.csv`; `sparc_reproduce.py`);
- the Λ_SC range (`LAMBDA_SC_UNSCREENED.json`);
- the λ_s solar-system table at the live a₀ (`LAMBDA_S_AT_LIVE_A0_2026-10-09.txt`);
- the live a₀ measurement (`A0_DISTANCE_CORRECTED_2026-09-16.json`; `scripts/a0_distance_corrected_reextract.py`, seeded bootstrap).

Five files must match byte for byte. Three are compared field by field to a stated relative tolerance (`scripts/compare_json_numeric.py`):
- the a₀ file, to 10⁻¹², because its bootstrap percentiles move in the last digit across numpy builds;
- `EFE_QUADRUPOLE_Q2.json` and `CASSINI_PARETO_SCAN.json`, to 10⁻⁸. Their values come out of L-BFGS-B fits, which stop once χ² improves by less than 2.2×10⁻⁹ in relative terms (scipy's default). A different BLAS thread count or CPU changes the summation order, so the fits can stop slightly apart. An offline run in a separate 2-vCPU machine on 2026-10-09 matched the other five files byte for byte, and these two to within 4.2×10⁻¹⁰ (`CHECKS.md`). Byte identity for them holds only on the machine that wrote them.

Every number quoted on this page is far coarser than these tolerances. The command then re-checks the generated benchmark tables (`scripts/sparc_benchmark_tables.py --check`). A full run on 2026-10-09, before the tolerance change, took 389 s, exited 0, and printed `REPRODUCED: 7 result files byte-identical and the a0 file equal to 1e-12`. After it, the offline run of `d154862` in the second machine (snapshot tree `047412d1`) exited 0 and printed `REPRODUCED: 5 result files byte-identical and 3 within tolerance (a0 1e-12; EFE, Cassini 1e-8)`.

## 1. SPARC rotation curves

Data: SPARC (Lelli, McGaugh & Schombert 2016). 171 of the 175 galaxies are used (the 4 with fewer than 5 points are dropped), 3,375 points. Both MOND rows use the same interpolating function, μ(x) = x/√(1+x²). They differ only in a0. Source: `02_galaxy_dynamics/PARAMETER_LEDGER.json` (`parameter_ledger.py`).

| model | a0 (m/s²) | tier 0: median χ²ν, no per-galaxy freedom | tier 1: median χ²ν, matched nuisance (Υ_d, Υ_b, f_D) |
|---|---|---|---|
| μ_std, a0 = cH₀/2π (declared anchor) | 1.042×10⁻¹⁰ | 11.08 | 3.36 (374 params) |
| μ_std, a0 fitted (literature) | 1.200×10⁻¹⁰ | 9.93 | 3.41 (374 params) |
| NFW halo | none | cannot run at tier 0 | 1.92 (716 params) |

At tier 0 the fitted a0 fits better. At tier 1 the two are tied. NFW, with 342 more parameters, fits better than both.

## 2. Out-of-domain solar-system extrapolation (archived; not a gate on the galaxy law)

> **Scope ruling (2026-11-21):** Under [`02_galaxy_dynamics/GALAXY_LAW_SCOPE_2026-11-21.md`](02_galaxy_dynamics/GALAXY_LAW_SCOPE_2026-11-21.md) and [`OUTSIDE_GALAXIES.md`](OUTSIDE_GALAXIES.md), `μ_std` with measured `a₀` is the galaxy law scored on galaxies, while ordinary general relativity holds outside galaxies. The Cassini external-field quadrupole `Q₂` is a solar-system measurement — **not** a gate on or failure of `μ_std`, `a₀`, or SPARC. The agent-written joint `Cassini × SPARC` requirement is **withdrawn** (Erratum `E-16`). The reproducible numbers below remain archived strictly as measurements of extrapolating the galaxy formula into the solar system; do **not** retune `μ_std` to move them.

- **Method.** Milgrom's QUMOND expression in the form of Hees et al. 2016 (MNRAS 455, 449), eq. 12.
- **Validation.** Hees 2016 Table 2 is reproduced: −q = 0.1001 against 0.10 for ν₂, and 0.1317 against 0.131 for ν̄₀.₅.
- **Extrapolation measurement.** At a0 = 1.042×10⁻¹⁰, extrapolating μ_std into the Milky Way external field at Saturn gives Q₂ = 1.67–1.72×10⁻²⁶ s⁻² for g_e = 1.9–2.4×10⁻¹⁰ m/s² (+4.6σ / +4.7σ against Hees et al. 2014, (3 ± 3)×10⁻²⁷, and +8.7σ against Park et al. 2026, arXiv:2602.17884, (1.6 ± 1.8)×10⁻²⁷; `15.40–19.89×10⁻²⁷ s⁻²` across the ±21% `g_e` Hill-sphere sweep in `audits/2026-11-21/HILL_SPHERE_EFE_BVP.json`). The McGaugh RAR shape gives +8.2σ / +10.8σ under the same extrapolation.
- **Agreement with prior work.** This reproduces Desmond (arXiv:2401.04796) as an out-of-domain extrapolation calculation, marking where Block 3 stops and Block 2 (ordinary GR) applies (`OUTSIDE_GALAXIES.md`). It is not a failure of the galaxy law.

Source: `02_galaxy_dynamics/EFE_QUADRUPOLE_Q2.json` (`efe_quadrupole_q2.py`). Write-up: `02_galaxy_dynamics/CASSINI_EFE_QUADRUPOLE_2026-09-27.md`.
