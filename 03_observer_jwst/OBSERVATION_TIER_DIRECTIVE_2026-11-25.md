## The two tiers

1. **Dynamics tier: a galaxy as a system.** The ITT action is applied at the galaxy's own level. This is the galaxy law (FRONT Block 3), scored on galaxies (`GALAXY_LAW_SCOPE_2026-11-21.md`). Ordinary GR holds outside galaxies (`OUTSIDE_GALAXIES.md`).
2. **Observation tier: how we see galaxies.** A **geometric optical effect** acts on the light that reaches us from galaxies. In RY's reading, it is what science has called dark matter. The JWST "impossible" early galaxies and other galactic anomalies are to be addressed at this tier, not by modifying dynamics at another scale.

## Consequences for current work

- **Wrong-tier work is deprioritized.** Relativistic MOND completions tested on satellites moving through the Milky Way, and screening chases in the solar system, are MOND questions at the wrong tier for ITT. Example: AeST branch selection, D7 (`exploration/d7_stage2/ARCLENGTH_RECORD_2026-11-25.md`). D7 stays `[O]`, but only as a statement about AeST; it is not a test of ITT.
- **O4 (JWST) belongs to the observation tier.** The quarantine on JWST language stays until a pre-registered observation-tier test produces a citable JSON.

## What the observation tier still needs (`[O]`, in order)

1. **A written optical map.** It must say how the information-tension geometry along the line of sight changes each observed quantity: redshift, brightness and luminosity distance, angular size, image magnification and shear (lensing), and the masses and ages inferred from them. Existing corpus pieces, none of them derived:
   - the tension factor T(χ) = 1 + 1/(χ/2) (`GOD_Formalization.lean`, `tension_factor`);
   - the 141.99 "boost", whose origin is traced to one galaxy's mass excess (CEERS-93316) and is not a derivation;
   - `jwst_clean_analysis.py` over 18 "impossible" galaxies: time windows resolve 7 of 18, and the mean needed factor is 1.34×.
2. **Boundary conditions any optical map must meet.** These come from data where the effect is already constrained. Example: the cosmic distance-duality relation D_L = (1+z)² D_A, tested with SNe against BAO at z < 2.3. Any optical brightening of distant sources must respect it there, or explain why it does not.
3. **Discriminating predictions, to be pre-registered before running.** These are places where an optical reading and a mass reading of "dark matter" disagree. Candidates:
   - lensing of gravitational waves against lensing of light from the same source;
   - how the JWST mass excess scales with line-of-sight environment (χ);
   - Bullet-type cluster offsets.
