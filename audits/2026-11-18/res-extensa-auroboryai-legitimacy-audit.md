**Two near-collisions that must be recorded, not smoothed over.**

1. `extended-works` cites an "exponential-family" screen `nu_6(y) = (1 - e^{-sqrt(y)})^{-1}` as the replacement for the killed `f(Q)` branch. This is a `nu`-function in the Newtonian variable `y = g_bar/a0`, not a `mu`-function in `x = g/a0`; the two are related by `mu(x) = 1/nu(y)` with `x = y*nu(y)`. It is therefore **not** the quarantined `mu = 1 - e^{-x}`. It must be labelled explicitly as a `nu`-function in every future citation, because the digit string invites exactly the confusion the quarantine exists to prevent.

2. The audit brief describes the Euclid DR1 protocol as "P1 magnification bias `T_1^(mu) = 1`, P2, P3 icosahedral `I_h` multipoles at `ell = 6, 10, 12, 20`". **No such protocol exists in either audited repository.** Auroboryai's frozen prediction record (`papers/euclid-predictions/shadow-ladder-euclid-dr1-predictions-paper.tex`, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24109539) defines `P1` as a lensing dipole asymmetry, `P2` as ten caustic vertex sky targets, and `P3` as two published shape fits. The words "magnification" and "icosahedral multipole" do not occur in any `.tex` in the repository. Res-Nova contains no Euclid file at all, and its own November audit notes that the DR1 high-level shear products are not yet released. The brief's version is reported here as unverified and is **not** used anywhere in Part 2.
