# EXPLORATORY: AeST Aether Field as a Relativistic Bohmian Foliation

**Status:** EXPLORATORY / SPECULATIVE. Not a derivation, not a claim about Res-Nova's
existing targets. A research lead surfaced 2026-09-12 from noticing that D6's own
documented superluminal vector-perturbation structure resembles the preferred-foliation
problem in relativistic pilot-wave (Bohmian) mechanics.
**Tags:** `[C]` cited literature only · `[O]` open — nothing here is proved or derived.

---

## 1. The resemblance that prompted this

`TARGET_D6_RELATIVISTIC_STABILITY.md` §4 documents that AeST's unit-timelike aether field
$A^\mu$ (Lagrange-multiplier constrained, `TARGET_D7` §1) lets vector perturbations
propagate superluminally while tensor modes stay locked at $c$ — i.e., $A^\mu$ defines a
preferred time-direction/foliation of spacetime at every point, without breaking the
observable light-cone structure.

Relativistic completions of Bohmian mechanics need exactly this kind of object: a
preferred foliation, so the theory's non-local guidance equation has a well-defined
"instant" to act on, while keeping observable predictions Lorentz-covariant.

## 2. What the literature actually contains `[C]`

- Dürr, Goldstein, Norsen, Struyve, Zanghì, "Can Bohmian mechanics be made relativistic?"
  *Proc. R. Soc. A* (2014), arXiv:1307.1714 — the foliation is **covariantly determined by
  the wavefunction/multi-particle current itself**: a unit timelike vector built from the
  quantum state, not an independently posited field.
- Dürr et al., "Relativistic Bohmian mechanics without a preferred foliation,"
  arXiv:1509.03463 — a later variant that removes even the wavefunction-derived foliation.
- Tumulka et al., "Relativistic-covariant Bohmian mechanics with proper foliation,"
  arXiv:1205.4102, and "Bohmian mechanics for a degenerate time foliation,"
  arXiv:1505.02844 — same family; foliation is derived/emergent from the quantum state,
  sometimes degenerate.
- Valentini's work distinguishes "frame" (state of motion) from "foliation" (simultaneity
  surfaces), but again ties the foliation to the hidden-variable/quantum-state dynamics,
  not to an independently dynamical geometric field.

## 3. The structural finding — surface resemblance, not the same object `[O]`

In every relativistic-Bohmian construction found, the preferred-foliation vector is a
**kinematic bookkeeping device**: no independent kinetic term, no Lagrange-multiplier
unit-norm constraint, no field equations of its own sourced by an action, no back-reaction
on the metric.

AeST's $A^\mu$ is the opposite: an independently **dynamical** field — unit-timelike via
Lagrange multiplier, carrying its own kinetic term built from $\nabla A$, sourced by and
back-reacting on the metric through its own stress-energy, propagating its own (possibly
superluminal) modes.

**No paper found connects Einstein-aether (Jacobson–Mattingly) or Skordis–Złośnik AeST
specifically to relativistic pilot-wave foliation construction.** Two literatures, disjoint,
no cross-citation found in the search performed.

## 4. What this actually means — the open door, not a dead end `[O]`

The resemblance is real (both solve "need a preferred time direction while keeping
observables covariant") but the two solutions occupy different structural slots: one is
derived-from-state, the other is independently-dynamical. **Nobody has tried substituting
a dynamical aether-type field for the wavefunction-derived foliation in a relativistic
Bohmian construction.** This is not a connection that's been made and found wanting — it's
a connection that has never been attempted, as far as this search found.

**Most promising concrete next step, if pursued:** take Dürr–Goldstein–Norsen–Struyve–Zanghì
2014 (arXiv:1307.1714) as the baseline Bohmian construction — it's the most explicit
"vector defines foliation" version in the family — and compare its foliation vector's role
in the guidance equation directly, equation-by-equation, against AeST's $A^\mu$ field
equations (`TARGET_D7` §1–§2). The question to answer: does substituting a dynamical,
action-sourced $A^\mu$ for DGZ's state-derived foliation vector break anything in either
theory, or does it compose cleanly?

**Explicitly not claimed:** that this connection exists, that it would work, that it says
anything about Res-Nova's actual open physics problems (D3/D5/D2's μ_std uniqueness gap).
This is a separate, unrelated research thread, logged because the resemblance was real and
worth a concrete write-up rather than a passing remark.
